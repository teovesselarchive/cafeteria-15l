# A Downtown comfort-food editorial

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A Downtown comfort-food editorial. Preserve this venue’s physical character and avoid a reusable restaurant-template opening.

### OWN-WORLD
Ivory #f3f0e7, brick #93462f, DM Serif display and DM Sans body. Full-bleed marquee room; ruled utility strip; asymmetric food and room spreads.

### STORY
Comfort food beneath its name in lights, followed by six actual dishes, the patio, and visit details. Eight homepage sections lead from first impression through menu, space, events, weekly programming, arrival and family links.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
The real marquee dining room fills the opening. Meatloaf. Marquee. 15th & L. sits low in giant serif type, followed by booking actions.

### FORM
Code-led, venue-specific photographic composition. Type scale, image placement and menu rhythm stay distinct across the five venues. Hero photographs move subtly with scrolling where useful; MIX alone has a pausable text strip. No repeated section-rise entrances. Reduced-motion removes motion. Text-only actions, keyboard gallery and click-to-load map preserve straightforward interaction.

### Evidence and limits
Facts: data/revision-facts.json. Real original photographs with provenance. Headlines and display story lines stay under eight words; no banned phrases or invented reviews, awards or quotes. The Impeccable engine was unavailable; concept-seed and detector gates remain unpassed. No seed, automated quality certification, approved comp or QUALITY BAR card is claimed. This written direction records the implemented fallback.
